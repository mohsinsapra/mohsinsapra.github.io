Data deletion instructions: Inbox Tool

Last updated: 8 November 2026

The Inbox Tool is a private tool that Muhammad Mohsin uses to read and reply to messages and comments on his own Instagram account, @mohsin.sapra. It stores message and comment text, usernames and Instagram IDs locally for up to 90 days. See the privacy policy.

How to request deletion

  1. Email mohsin.sapra@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my data (Inbox Tool)", or send a DM to @mohsin.sapra saying "delete my data".
  2. Include your Instagram username so I can find your records.
  3. Within 30 days I delete every stored message, comment and ID linked to you from the tool's storage and confirm by reply.

If you only want to stop the tool accessing your data, you can also block or restrict @mohsin.sapra on Instagram. Messages inside Instagram itself can be deleted ("unsent") in the Instagram app.
