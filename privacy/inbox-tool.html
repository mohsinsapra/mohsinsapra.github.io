Privacy Policy: Inbox Tool (grafana-analytics-dashboard)

Last updated: 8 November 2026

The Inbox Tool is a private, internal tool run by Muhammad Mohsin (Instagram @mohsin.sapra), an individual creator based in Sweden. It helps me read and reply to direct messages and comments on my own Instagram account. It is not offered to the public and no other Instagram accounts are connected to it.

What data the tool accesses

  • Direct messages sent to @mohsin.sapra and my replies to them (message text, time, the sender's Instagram username and app-scoped ID).
  • Comments on posts of @mohsin.sapra and my replies (comment text, time, the commenter's username).
  • Basic profile data of my own account (username, account ID) needed to log in.

How the data is used

Only to show me new messages and comments and to send replies on my account. Replies are drafted with software help and reviewed by me; only short, pre-approved standard answers are sent automatically. The data is not used for advertising, profiling, analytics about individuals, or training AI models.

Where it is stored and for how long

Data is stored only on my own computer (and my own private, access-controlled storage). Message and comment logs are kept for up to 90 days so I can follow up on conversations, then deleted. Access tokens are stored in my computer's encrypted keychain.

Sharing

I do not sell, rent or share this data with anyone. It is not transferred to third parties, except that drafting may be done with the help of an AI service provider acting only on my instructions, without keeping the data for its own purposes.

Your rights and deleting your data

If you have messaged or commented on @mohsin.sapra and want the data this tool holds about you deleted, see Data deletion instructions, or email mohsin.sapra@gmail.com. You may also ask what data I hold about you. I will reply within 30 days. Messages stay inside Instagram itself according to Instagram's own policies; you can delete them there too.
