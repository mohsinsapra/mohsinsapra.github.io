Privacy notice: tools

Last updated: 22 November 2026. This covers the small tools at mohsin.se/tools, such as AppSweep. The tools themselves run on your Mac.

Who is responsible

Muhammad Mohsin, an individual based in Sweden. Contact: see the contact section of mohsin.se. Please write in English.

When you download a tool

I store a download log with:

  • your country
  • the day
  • a hashed version of your IP address that cannot be reversed (the raw IP address is not stored)
  • which file you downloaded
  • your browser's user agent family

Purpose: counting how many people use each tool and spotting abuse. Retention: 12 months.

When you subscribe for updates

If you tick the consent box and submit your email, I store:

  • your email address
  • the time you gave consent
  • the page you subscribed from
  • your country

Purpose: sending updates about my tools and announcing new tools. Legal basis: your consent. Retention: until you unsubscribe or ask me to delete it.

Unsubscribe or deletion

You can unsubscribe at any time, and you can ask me to delete your data, by contacting me through the contact section of mohsin.se. Please write in English and use the email address you subscribed with.

Who processes the data

The data is processed on Cloudflare's infrastructure. I do not sell your data and I do not share it with anyone else.

Inside the AppSweep app

To show each app's App Store category and real icon, AppSweep looks the apps up through Apple's public App Store lookup. For that, the bundle ids of the apps on your iPhone are sent to Apple. This happens from the AppSweep app on your Mac, not from this website. Apps that are not on the App Store show as Other. Apple's handling of those requests is governed by Apple's own privacy policy.

Site analytics

Pages on mohsin.se use Google Analytics only if you accept the cookie banner.
