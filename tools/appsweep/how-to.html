How to use AppSweep

Remove many iPhone apps at once from your Mac, in a few minutes.

Before you start

  • A Mac with macOS 13 or later
  • A USB cable for your iPhone
  • Homebrew (see below if you do not have it)

Read this first: removing an app deletes its data and cannot be undone. Apple's own apps cannot be removed.

Step by step

  1. Install AppSweepOpen Terminal and run:
    brew install mohsinsapra/tools/appsweep
    Homebrew also installs the helper tools libimobiledevice and ideviceinstaller for you.
  2. Connect your iPhonePlug it into your Mac with a USB cable. Unlock the iPhone and tap Trust when it asks.
  3. Start AppSweepIn Terminal run:
    appsweep
  4. Tick the apps you want goneAppSweep shows the apps on your iPhone. Tick each one you want to remove and check the list once more.
  5. Press UninstallThe ticked apps are removed from the iPhone.

Pick faster: categories, sizes and Keep

  • Use the category filter and tick a whole category at once, for example all Health & Fitness apps.
  • Sort by size, and check the "frees about X GB" figure for your selection.
  • Star the apps you want to keep. Starred apps are protected from select-all.

How to find apps you never open

iOS does not give any tool, including AppSweep, the date an app was last used. Use the iPhone itself:

  1. On the iPhone open Settings > General > iPhone Storage. Each app shows Last Used.
  2. Note the names of the apps you have not opened for a long time.
  3. In AppSweep, tick those apps.

The saved-data size in AppSweep is only a hint. A small size does not mean an app is unused.

If you downloaded the .zip instead

Downloaded copies are not notarized by Apple. On first start, right-click the app and choose Open, or run:

xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/AppSweep.app

Installing with Homebrew avoids this.

Troubleshooting

My iPhone is not found

  • Unlock the iPhone and keep the screen on.
  • Tap Trust on the iPhone when the prompt appears. If you tapped Don't Trust earlier, unplug and plug the cable in again to get the prompt back.
  • Try another cable or another USB port.
  • Check whether your Mac can see the iPhone. In Terminal run idevice_id -l. It should print your iPhone's ID. If it prints nothing, the Mac does not see the iPhone yet.

An app fails to remove

  • A Screen Time restriction on the iPhone can block removing apps. Check the restrictions and try again.
  • The app may be a managed app (installed by an employer or school), which cannot be removed this way.
  • Apple's own apps cannot be removed.

Homebrew is not installed

Go to brew.sh and copy the one-line install command into Terminal. When it finishes, run the AppSweep install command again.

Uninstall AppSweep

To remove AppSweep from your Mac:

brew uninstall appsweep
