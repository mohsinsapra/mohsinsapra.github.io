AppSweep

Remove many iPhone apps at once from your Mac.

Install

Install with Homebrew, then start it.

brew install mohsinsapra/tools/appsweep

Then start it with:

appsweep
Download .zip
Downloaded copies are not notarized by Apple. On first start, right-click the app and choose Open, or run xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/AppSweep.app. Installing with Homebrew avoids this.

Downloads are logged as described in the tools privacy notice. Need help? See the how-to guide.

How it works

  1. Plug in your iPhoneConnect it to your Mac with a USB cable and tap Trust on the iPhone.
  2. Tick the appsAppSweep lists the apps on your iPhone. Tick every app you want gone.
  3. Press UninstallAll the ticked apps are removed in one go.

What you can see and do

  • Categories and icons: each app shows its App Store category and real icon. Filter by category and tick a whole category at once, for example all Health & Fitness apps. Apps that are not on the App Store show as Other.
  • Sizes: see each app's size and saved-data size, sort by size, and see "frees about X GB" for your selection.
  • Keep star: star the apps that matter so they are protected from select-all.

Category and icon data come from Apple's public App Store lookup, so the bundle ids of your apps are sent to Apple for that. More in the tools privacy notice.

Requirements

  • Any Mac with macOS 13 or later
  • A USB cable for your iPhone
  • Homebrew installs the helper tools libimobiledevice and ideviceinstaller automatically

Honest limits

  • Apple's own apps cannot be removed.
  • Removing an app deletes its data.
  • A removal cannot be undone.
  • It works with the iPhone connected by cable.

Questions

Is AppSweep free?

Yes. AppSweep is a free tool.

Which Macs does it work on?

Any Mac running macOS 13 or later. You also need a USB cable to connect your iPhone.

What does Homebrew install for me?

Installing with Homebrew also installs the helper tools libimobiledevice and ideviceinstaller automatically, so there is nothing else to set up.

Can I remove Apple's own apps?

No. Apple's own apps cannot be removed with AppSweep.

Does removing an app delete its data?

Yes. Removing an app deletes the data stored in it.

Can I undo a removal?

No. A removal cannot be undone, so check your ticked apps before you press Uninstall.

Does it work without a cable?

No. AppSweep works with the iPhone connected to your Mac by cable.

Does AppSweep show categories and icons?

Yes. It shows each app's App Store category and real icon, lets you filter by category and tick a whole category at once, for example all Health & Fitness apps. This data comes from Apple's public App Store lookup, so the bundle ids of your apps are sent to Apple for that lookup. Apps that are not on the App Store show as Other.

Can AppSweep show when I last used an app?

No. iOS does not give any tool the last used date. See the how-to guide for how to find apps you never open with the iPhone's own storage screen.

Can I protect apps from select-all?

Yes. Each app has a Keep star, and starred apps are protected from select-all.

Why does macOS warn me about the downloaded .zip?

The downloaded copy is not notarized by Apple. On first start, right-click the app and choose Open, or run the xattr command shown in the install box. Installing with Homebrew avoids this.
