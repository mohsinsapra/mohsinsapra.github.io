How to delete multiple apps on iPhone at once (what actually works)

By Muhammad Mohsin, maker of AppSweep. Published 22 November 2026.

If you want to clear out fifty apps, the iPhone makes you do it fifty times. This guide answers the questions people actually ask about deleting many apps, shows what works on the phone itself, and then compares the desktop tools that can do it in one go. I make one of them (AppSweep), so I say where the others are the better choice.

Quick answer: on the iPhone you delete one app at a time. To remove many at once, connect the phone to a computer and use a tool that lets you tick several apps. See which one to use.

Can you delete multiple apps on iPhone at once?

On the phone, no: deleting is done one app at a time. In Apple's guide for removing apps there is no multi-select step (checked in Apple's iOS 18 guide).

The steps Apple documents are per app: touch and hold the app on the Home Screen and choose Remove App, or touch and hold it in the App Library and choose Delete App. You can read them in Apple's iPhone User Guide, Remove or delete apps. Your iOS version may look slightly different, so if you see a bulk option on your phone, use it.

What is the fastest native way to delete apps?

All three built-in routes are one app per confirmation. Pick the one that matches how you find the apps.

Home Screen (jiggle mode)

Touch and hold an app, choose Remove App, then Delete App. Apple also offers Remove from Home Screen, which keeps the app in your App Library. This is fine for a handful of apps you can see.

App Library

Swipe past your last Home Screen page, touch and hold an app, and choose Delete App, then confirm. The App Library groups apps by category, which helps when you cannot remember where something lives.

Settings > General > iPhone Storage

This list shows how much space each app takes, so it is the best place to find large apps. Open an app in the list and its page offers to offload or delete it. Apple describes the storage screen in How to check the storage on your iPhone and iPad. It is still one app at a time.

Can I delete apps from a computer?

Yes. Desktop tools can list the apps on a connected iPhone and uninstall the ones you select, including several at a time. The phone has to be connected by USB, unlocked and trusted.

The tools differ in price, platform and how much else they do. I compare them in the next section, and in more detail in AppSweep vs iMazing vs 3uTools vs Apple Configurator.

What is the difference between Delete and Offload?

Delete removes the app. Offload frees the storage used by the app but keeps its documents and data.

That second sentence is Apple's own wording on its iPhone storage page. Offload is the safer choice for an app you might want back with your progress intact. Delete is the right choice when you are sure. Apple notes that removing an app can also remove its related user data, so back up anything you need first. AppSweep removes apps through the same iOS installation service, so treat its removals the same way.

Why can't I delete an app on my iPhone?

Usually because of a Screen Time restriction, or because the phone is managed by a school or employer.

  • Screen Time: a restriction can block deleting apps. Look in your Screen Time settings under content and privacy restrictions.
  • Managed device: an organisation can block removal of the apps it installed. Only the administrator can change that.
  • Built-in app: some Apple apps are part of iOS and have no remove option.

A desktop tool does not bypass these rules. If the phone refuses, the tool will fail too.

Can I delete built-in Apple apps?

Some, not all. If touching and holding an app shows no remove option, it is part of iOS.

For the ones that can be removed, you can get them back from the App Store. I would not rely on a desktop tool to remove system apps, and AppSweep is built for the apps you installed yourself.

How do I get back an app I deleted?

Search for it in the App Store and download it again, if it is still available there.

Apple's guide confirms that removed apps can be redownloaded. Your purchases stay tied to your Apple Account. Data stored only inside the app may not come back, which is why a backup matters before a big clean-up.

Do I need to jailbreak to delete apps from a computer?

No. Desktop tools use the same USB service that other iPhone management tools use. The phone must be unlocked and must trust the computer.

AppSweep works this way. When you connect the phone, iOS asks whether to trust the computer, and you need to say yes and enter your passcode. Nothing about the phone is modified.

Desktop tools that can remove many apps

iMazing

iMazing is a large iPhone manager for Mac and Windows. Its guide to deleting apps says to select one or more apps from the list and click Uninstall. It also does backups, transfers and much more. Licensing is subscription-based, see their site for details.

3uTools

3uTools has downloads for Windows, macOS and Linux, and its site says it is free for all users. Its official tutorial says to select the apps you want to delete and click Uninstall. It is a broad toolbox, not only an app remover.

AppSweep

AppSweep is my free Mac tool with one job. It lists the apps on your iPhone by name, lets you filter by App Store category, shows sizes, lets you tick many apps and uninstall them together. A Keep star marks apps that must never be removed. Install it with brew install mohsinsapra/tools/appsweep and read the how-to.
